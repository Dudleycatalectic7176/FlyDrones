## ✨ What Is FlyDrones?

FlyDrones is a one-of-a-kind application that lets you fly a drone using a **simulated fruit fly brain**. That's right — instead of a remote control, your drone's movements are guided by a digital copy of a real fly's neural connections.

The magic works like this:

**Camera → Fly Eyes → Spiking Brain → Descending Neurons → Drone**

- Your computer camera acts as the fly's eyes.
- The app processes what it sees through a simulated brain structure.
- That brain sends commands to your drone.
- Your drone responds instantly.

You can see the whole thing happening in a **live 3D demo** right in your browser. No special equipment needed — just your computer and a compatible drone.

## 📚 How Does the Fly Brain Work?

The fruit fly (Drosophila melanogaster) has about 100,000 neurons. That's tiny compared to a human brain, but it's enough to control complex flight maneuvers with lightning speed.

FlyDrones uses a **complete wiring diagram** (called a connectome) of the male fruit fly's central nervous system. This means every connection between neurons is mapped and simulated.

Here's what happens in real time:

1. **Visual Input** — The camera captures the world around you.
2. **Eye Processing** — Simulated fly eyes translate the image into neural signals.
3. **Brain Simulation** — A spiking neural network processes those signals using the connectome.
4. **Motor Commands** — Descending neurons fire, sending movement instructions.
5. **Drone Response** — Your drone executes the commands instantly.

The result is a flying machine that reacts the way a fly would — fast, agile, and responsive.
